================================================================================
FEATURED NEIGHBOURHOODS IN SINGAPORE
================================================================================

Singapore is a tapestry of vibrant, diverse neighbourhoods, each with its own unique 
character, heritage, architectural charm, and modern lifestyle offerings. Explore the 
top featured enclaves across the island.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CHINATOWN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Overview:
  A bustling historic precinct where ancient traditions blend seamlessly with trendy 
  lifestyle spots.
* Highlights:
  - Landmarks: Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, Sri Mariamman Temple, and Masjid Jamae.
  - Food: Chinatown Complex Food Centre and Maxwell Food Centre.
  - Shopping & Lifestyle: Traditional medicine shops, tea houses (Tea Chapter), 
    and lively night markets.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. KAMPONG GELAM & HAJI LANE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Overview:
  Singapore's historic Malay-Arab quarter, recognized for its rich heritage, vibrant 
  street art, and bohemian vibe.
* Highlights:
  - Landmarks: Sultan Mosque and Malay Heritage Centre.
  - Haji Lane: Famous for hip indie boutiques, vintage clothing shops, cafes, and 
    colorful murals.
  - Food: Authentic Middle Eastern and Malay cuisines along Arab Street and Bussorah Street.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. LITTLE INDIA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Overview:
  A colorful and sensory-rich enclave showcasing Singapore’s Indian heritage and community.
* Highlights:
  - Landmarks: Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple and House of Tan Teng Niah.
  - Shopping: Mustafa Centre (a 24/7 mega department store) and Tekka Centre market.
  - Cuisine: Authentic South and North Indian delicacies, banana leaf rice, and traditional sweets.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. KATONG & JOO CHIAT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Overview:
  The heartland of Peranakan (Straits Chinese) culture, famous for its pastel-colored 
  heritage shophouses and rich culinary scene.
* Highlights:
  - Heritage Shophouses: Intricate tilework, floral motifs, and distinctive architecture.
  - Culture & Crafts: Kim Choo Kueh Chang for beaded slippers, traditional crafts, and kebayas.
  - Food: Famous Katong Laksa and Nyonya kueh.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. TIONG BAHRU
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Overview:
  One of Singapore's oldest housing estates, transformed into a charming art-deco 
  neighbourhood favored by creatives.
* Highlights:
  - Architecture: Streamline Moderne art-deco residential blocks.
  - Lifestyle: Independent bookstores, record shops, and third-wave cafes.
  - Food: Tiong Bahru Market for classic local hawker breakfasts.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. CIVIC DISTRICT & MARINA BAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Overview:
  The birth-place of modern Singapore, featuring grand colonial architecture set against 
  the futuristic Marina Bay skyline.
* Highlights:
  - Museums & Culture: National Gallery Singapore, Asian Civilisations Museum, and Esplanade.
  - Skyline Icons: Marina Bay Sands, Merlion Park, and Gardens by the Bay.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. DEMPSEY HILL & HOLLAND VILLAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Overview:
  Lush, upscale enclaves popular for relaxed lifestyle dining, European-style cafes, 
  and bohemian nightlife.
* Highlights:
  - Dempsey Hill: Former British military barracks turned into tranquil fine dining and 
    lifestyle hubs.
  - Holland Village: Lively dining spots, craft beer bars, and artisan bakeries.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. NEW BAHRU (RIVER VALLEY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Overview:
  A newly reimagined creative lifestyle precinct located at a former high school site.
* Highlights:
  - Showcasing homegrown Singaporean brands, design pop-ups, artisanal workshops, and 
    contemporary dining concepts.
