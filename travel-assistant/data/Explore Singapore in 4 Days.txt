================================================================================
4 DAYS IN SINGAPORE ITINERARY
================================================================================

Discover the perfect blend of rich culture, futuristic architecture, world-class 
dining, and island entertainment with this comprehensive four-day guide to 
exploring Singapore.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DAY 1: HERITAGE, HISTORIC ENCLAVES & ICONIC SKYLINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Kampong Gelam & Sultan Mosque
  Start your journey in one of Singapore's oldest urban quarters. Explore 
  vibrant street art along Haji Lane, browse traditional textiles, and visit 
  the golden-domed Sultan Mosque.

* Civic District & Merlion Park
  Stroll through Singapore's historic heart, taking in colonial architecture at 
  the National Gallery Singapore. Walk to Merlion Park for iconic photo 
  opportunities with Singapore's legendary mascot overlooking Marina Bay.

* Gardens by the Bay
  Spend the late afternoon in the Flower Dome and Cloud Forest cooled conservatories. 
  In the evening, head to the Supertree Grove to witness the mesmerizing Garden 
  Rhapsody light and sound show.

* Marina Bay Sands & Spectra Light Show
  Finish Day 1 on the SkyPark Observation Deck for panoramic city lights, or 
  watch the Spectra light and water fountain show along the Bayfront Promenade.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DAY 2: CULTURAL NEIGHBORHOODS & LOCAL FLAVORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Katong & Joo Chiat
  Discover Peranakan heritage in Katong. Admire colorful pastel shophouses, 
  shop for intricate beadwork, and taste local delicacies such as Katong Laksa.

* Chinatown & Buddha Tooth Relic Temple
  Immerse yourself in Chinatown's bustling streets. Visit the grand Buddha 
  Tooth Relic Temple, explore traditional medicine shops, and enjoy authentic 
  hawker food at Maxwell Food Centre.

* Little India & Serangoon Road
  Experience a burst of color and fragrance along Serangoon Road. Visit the 
  historic Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple and shop for spice blends, sarees, and 
  jewelry.

* Nightlife along Singapore River (Clarke Quay / Boat Quay)
  Cap off the day with a relaxing bumboat cruise or dinner along the water in 
  Clarke Quay or Robertson Quay.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DAY 3: ISLAND ESCAPE & SENTOSA ENTERTAINMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Sentosa Island Arrival via Cable Car
  Take the Mount Faber Cable Car across to Sentosa Island, enjoying 360-degree 
  views of the city skyline and Singapore Harbor.

* Universal Studios Singapore
  Spend the day enjoying thrilling rides, live performances, and themed zones 
  featuring Transformers, Minions, and Jurassic Park.

* S.E.A. Aquarium & Beaches
  Discover marine life at one of the world's largest aquariums, then relax at 
  Siloso Beach, Palawan Beach, or Tanjong Beach.

* Sunset Drinks & Coastal Dining
  Enjoy dinner and cocktails at vibrant beach clubs like Tanjong Beach Club or 
  along Quayside Isle.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DAY 4: NATURE, LIFESTYLE & SHOPPING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Singapore Botanic Gardens
  Start the morning at Singapore's UNESCO World Heritage site. Wander through 
  the lush greenery and visit the stunning National Orchid Garden.

* Orchard Road Shopping
  Spend the afternoon along Singapore's premier shopping belt, featuring major 
  retail hubs such as ION Orchard, Design Orchard, and Ngee Ann City.

* Jewel Changi Airport
  Wrap up your trip at Changi Airport. Experience the iconic HSBC Rain Vortex 
  (the world's tallest indoor waterfall), Canopy Park, and world-class retail 
  and dining before your flight.
