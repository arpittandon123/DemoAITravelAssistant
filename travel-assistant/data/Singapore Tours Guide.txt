================================================================================
SINGAPORE TOURS GUIDE
================================================================================

Discover Singapore through a wide variety of tours, from heritage walks and street 
food trails to interactive gamified puzzle hunts and mystery outdoor escape games.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. GENERAL SINGAPORE TOURS OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Cultural & Heritage Tours
  - About Mr Lee Kuan Yew (4 hours | S$140/pax): Uncover the private life, health 
    discipline, and personal story of Singapore's founding Prime Minister.
  - Culture, Art and Architecture Private Tour (2.5 hours | S$62/pax): Explore 
    Chinatown and the Civic District through historic public art and architecture.
  - Disappearing Trades (4 hours | S$63/pax): Visit old-school businesses including 
    traditional bakeries, ancestral paper money shops, and coffee roasters.
  - Tea & Tiffin: Cultural & Food Tour of Little India (2.5 hours | S$68/pax): 
    Immerse in the vibrant flavors, traditions, and immigrant history of Little India.
  - Growing Up: A Mural Walk Through Chinatown (3 hours | S$60/pax): Walk through 
    Yip Yew Chong's detailed heritage murals portraying nostalgia and history.

* Specialized & Scenic Tours
  - Gin, Tonic & the Empire Tour (2.5 hours | S$75/pax): Discover ties between WWII history, 
    coastal forts, and gin distilling.
  - Historical Singapore Bicycle Tour (4 hours | S$80/pax): Cycle past iconic landmarks, 
    Formula 1 track sections, and historic riverside spots.
  - Singapore Uncut (2.5 hours | S$50/pax): Relive history off the beaten path through 
    hidden alleyways and local narratives.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. FOOD & DINING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Everyday Street Art + Street Food Tour (3.5 hours | S$599/6pax): Discover Singapore through a walking tour that blends bold street food with vibrant urban art.
  - Night Food Tour (3 hours | S$95/pax): Navigate Geylang's streets and discover a treasure trove of food gems.
  - Market + Street Food Tour (3-4 hours | S$599/6pax): Visit lively wet market followed by brunch. Explore a colorful Malay enclave then unwind in a private Peranakan home with treats and stories.
  - Market to Table Culinary Tour Experience (3 hours | S$129/pax): Bargain like a local at a wet market then whip up local dishes like Laksa.
  - Nocturnal Games (3 hours | S$150/pax): Start your evening with local delights in Chinatown, then test your gin-making skills at the Spiffy Dapper.
  - Singapore Street Food and Night Tour (3.5 hours | S$80/pax): Unwind evening in vibrant food streets, explore riverside spots and lively neighbourhoods, then end at Lau Pa Sat with smoky stay and sweeping skyline views.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Gamified & Adventure Tours
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Chinatown Murders (2 hours | S$55/pax): Channel your inner Sherlock and track down a serial killer in Chinatown! In an outdoor game, you'll travel back in time and meet characters who will share stories about Chinatown's past.
- Creepy Tales of Singapore (4 hours | S$150/pax): Ghost hunters, this one's for you! Explore some of Singapore's spine-tingling places with 'ghost-hunting' equipment, including a Chinese cemetery and a former coastal gun battery.
- Japanese Occupation Adventure Trails (3 hours | S$30/pax): Step back to relive Singapore's turbulent past. This story-driven walk follows wartime heroes through historic sites and secret safehouses as you decode messages in a race to change history.
- The Fortune Teller’s Legacy: Tales & Tastes of Chinatown (3 hours | S$65/pax): Solve riddles on this self-guided Chinatown adventure inspired by fictional fortune teller Madam Sampan. Explore heritage spots, sample local treats, and piece together her lost Wheel of Fortune along the way.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. Instagrammable Tours
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- #Instawalk: Bugis, Waterloo, Kampong Glam (2-3 hours | S$60/pax): Get your cameras ready! From Bugis to Kampong Gelam, you'll be spoilt for choice when picking the next picture for your socials.
- Joo Chiat + Katong - Nyonyas, Babas and Peranakans (1-2 hours | S$198/pax): Feel the wind in your face as you explore Singapore's Peranakan enclave on a vintage Vespa sidecar.
- Zoo-Declassified! (2.5 hours | S$35/pax): Satisfy your curiosity about the Singapore Zoo and its animals on this tour. You will get answers to questions about its world-famous "open zoo" design and interesting animal facts.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. Sustainability Tours
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Naturalist Night Adventure (2 hours | S$79/pax): As night falls, uncover Singapore's wild side on a guided forest walk. Spot giant ants, glowing scorpions and hidden creatures, while learning how nature's systems quietly shape the undergrowth.
- Night Kayak Fishing Experience (6-7 hours | S$149.90/pax): Paddle into calm waters on a guided night fishing trip on a leg-powered kayak. Chase fishes under the stars and end the evening on Pulau Ubin with your catch cooked fresh for a satisfying close to the day.
- Pulau Ubin Island Boat Tour + Kelong Visit (3 hours | S$135/pax): Set sail on a 3-hour boat tour along Singapore's northeastern coast. Drift past mangroves, visit floating fish farms, and appreciate Pulau Ubin from a new angle. Learn about sustainable aquaculture and enjoy a meal at a rustic kelong.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. Wellness Tours
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Finding Nemo - East Coast (4 hours | S$99/pax): Try fishing with a view on your next visit to Singapore. If you're looking to unwind or catch your dinner, this experience is perfect for beginners and families with kids.
- Intertidal Exploration (2 hours | S$79/pax): Get ready for an exciting journey of discovery! Catch sea creatures up close and learn about their behavioural adaptations and ecology that you might not have noticed before.
- Kayak Sailing (4 hours | S$129.90/pax): Embark on a kayak sailing trip combining wind and paddle power. Glide past mangroves toward Pulau Ubin, spot native wildlife, and end with a seafood feast at the island's last floating restaurant.
- My Bay Experience - History Wanderers (2.5 hours | S$75/pax): Paddle past Kallang Basin, Merdeka Bridge and the Sports Hub as stories unfold from colonial shores to modern skylines, offering a reflective view of the city's waterfront history.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. Free Tours
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- "Balik Kampong" Walk through Kampong Glam (3 hours | Free): Explore a hidden side of Singapore that most tourists never see. Wander through graffiti-covered streets, step inside the palace grounds of former kings and princes, and uncover gems in old shopping districts.
- City Sights Tour (2.5 hours | Free): Take in the sights of Singapore's cityscape at Marina Bay and marvel at key landmarks, from the Merlion to Gardens by the Bay. This tour will take you on a visual journey and leave you in awe.
- Heritage and Culture Tour (2.5 hours | Free): Get whisked away into Singapore's rich cultural heritage. This tour will start at the Civic District, uncovering traces of our colonial past before heading to Chinatown and Kampong Gelam.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. Accessible Tours
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Enabling Village Tour – Wheelchair friendly tours led by PWDs (2 hours | S$50/pax): Join us on a transformative journey through the Enabling Village, where inclusivity and empowerment take the centre stage. Our wheelchair friendly tour, led by a guide with disability, offers a unique experience in the heartlands of Singapore.
- Sentosa Wheel Venture Accessibility Tour (5 hours | S$120/pax): Calling all adventurers, thrill-seekers, and anyone who loves fun, regardless of mobility! Get ready to embark on a five-hour whirlwind adventure through the dazzling island of Sentosa, designed especially for our amazing friends on wheels.
