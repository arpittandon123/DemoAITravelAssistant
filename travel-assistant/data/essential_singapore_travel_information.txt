================================================================================
ESSENTIAL SINGAPORE TRAVEL INFORMATION
================================================================================

Prepare for an unforgettable journey to Singapore – get important information, 
tips and advice to maximize your experience and make the most of your visit.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GETTING AROUND SINGAPORE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Public Transport
  Singapore's public transport system is fast and efficient. The MRT (Mass 
  Rapid Transit) and bus systems have an extensive network of routes that will 
  help you zip around the city. Plan your journey with OneMap to find 
  accessible routes, nearby amenities, and convenient ways to get around.

* Ways to Pay
  There are many ways to pay for your public transport rides. You can use 
  contactless bank cards, mobile wallets, concession cards, EZ-Link cards, 
  cash, or a Singapore Tourist Pass — which offers all-day unlimited travel.

* Taxi or Private Hire Car
  Taxis or private hire cars offer convenience and comfort. You can flag a 
  taxi along the street or at designated taxi stands, or book a ride anytime 
  via mobile applications.

* Accessibility Options
  All MRTs and public buses are wheelchair accessible and open strollers are 
  allowed. Please approach train staff or bus captains if you need assistance.

* Mass Rapid Transit (MRT) Map
  For convenient navigation throughout the city, download the MyTransport App 
  or download the Singapore MRT map for a comprehensive overview of the system.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TRAVEL TIPS BEFORE VISITING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Weather & Timezone
  Enjoy summer all year-round with Singapore's tropical climate (25-33°C). 
  Dress in light, airy clothing and bring along an umbrella in case of 
  occasional rainfall. Singapore Standard Time is in the GMT +8 time zone.

* Visa & Entry Requirements
  While the majority of visitors do not require a visa, check the latest 
  requirements and advisories for accurate information to ensure a smooth 
  entry to Singapore.

* Smoking Area
  Smoking is prohibited in buildings, retail food establishments, transport 
  nodes, public service vehicles, and sports facilities. Designated smoking 
  areas are marked with notices or signs.

* E-vaporisers
  The purchase, possession, or use of e-vaporisers, including e-vaporiser 
  products, is illegal in Singapore. Offenders face penalties including 
  composition fines or prosecution. Visitors caught using e-vaporisers laced 
  with drugs may be refused entry or banned.

* Safe Drinking Water
  Tap water in Singapore surpasses World Health Organization standards. You 
  can drink water straight from the tap or easily purchase bottled water.

* Power Plug
  The standard electrical current used in Singapore is 220-240 volts AC (50 Hz). 
  Power plugs with three square prongs (Type G) are used.

* Language
  You can communicate in English with Singaporeans, who are mostly fluent in it. 
  Many also speak Mandarin Chinese, Malay, or Tamil, as well as Singlish (a 
  unique colloquial form of English).

* Useful Numbers
  - Police: 999
  - Ambulance & Fire Brigade: 995
  - Flight Information: 1800 542 4422

* Prayer Facilities
  Singapore is a Muslim-friendly destination with prayer facilities available 
  at major hotels, attractions, and mosques around the city.

* Wi-Fi
  Visitors can access free Wi-Fi at any Wireless@SGx hotspot with foreign 
  mobile numbers by downloading the Wireless@SGx App.

* Singapore Visitor Centre
  Drop by any Singapore Visitor Centre to plan itineraries, get tips, purchase 
  sightseeing tickets, book accommodation, or pick up souvenirs.

* Public Dining Places
  It is an offence to leave trays and litter on tables at hawker centres, food 
  courts, and coffeeshops. Return your trays and crockery at return stations.
