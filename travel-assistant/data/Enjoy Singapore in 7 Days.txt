================================================================================
ENJOY SINGAPORE IN 7 DAYS
================================================================================

Immerse yourself in a week-long journey through Singapore, discovering iconic 
modern marvels, action-packed island adventures, rich cultural enclaves, and 
lush nature spots.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DAY 1: URBAN NATURE, MODERN MARVELS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Marina Bay
  An iconic district in Singapore that blends nature, arts, and shopping.

* Gardens by the Bay
  Spend the day marveling at the flora on display at the Flower Dome, and explore 
  the mist-filled landscape of the Cloud Forest.

* Marina Bay Sands & The Shoppes
  Explore floors of luxury retailers, boasting the largest collection of coveted 
  designer boutiques in Asia.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DAY 2: THRILLS AND SPILLS AT SENTOSA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Sentosa Island
  Known for its white beaches, family-friendly activities, sun, sand, and surf 
  located off Singapore's southern shore.

* Universal Studios Singapore
  Experience the magic of the movies at this theme park featuring exhilarating 
  rides across six movie-themed districts.

* Singapore Oceanarium / S.E.A. Aquarium
  Embark on an odyssey beneath the waves and discover captivating marine species 
  from the deep.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DAY 3: CULTURE AND HERITAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Civic District
  Singapore's rich colonial history and modern future intertwine, where historical 
  museums sit alongside towering skyscrapers.

* National Museum of Singapore
  Explore Singapore's culture and heritage at its grandest and oldest museum, 
  where history comes alive.

* National Gallery Singapore
  Housed in the former City Hall and Supreme Court buildings, boasting over 8,000 
  works of art.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DAY 4: WILDLIFE AWAITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Mandai Wildlife Reserve
  Make your way to Northern Singapore to ignite your love for nature with majestic 
  animals and lush greenery.

* Bird Paradise
  Home to over 3,500 birds, featuring immersive walkthrough aviaries reflecting 
  global habitats.

* Singapore Zoo
  Experience the wonders of wildlife, from furry and adorable animals to majestic 
  predators in open-concept habitats.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DAY 5: HIP MEETS HERITAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Chinatown
  Take in the vibrant sights and sounds, visiting the Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, 
  Masjid Jamae Mosque, and Sri Mariamman Temple.

* Chinatown Complex Food Centre
  Taste various cultures from sweet to savoury at the largest hawker centre in 
  Singapore.

* Tong Heng Traditional Cantonese Pastries
  Sample famous candied melon pastries, savoury buns, and signature egg tarts.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DAY 6: A MIX OF EVERYTHING – NATURE, SHOPPING, PARTY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Singapore Botanic Gardens
  Spend the morning amidst nature and tranquility at this UNESCO World Heritage Site.

* National Orchid Garden
  Nestled within the Botanic Gardens, featuring the largest orchid display in the 
  world with over 60,000 plants and orchids.

* The Halia
  Enjoy brunch amidst the one-hectare Ginger Garden, specializing in contemporary 
  European cuisine.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DAY 7: PERANAKAN DELIGHTS AND RICH CULTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Katong-Joo Chiat
  Discover rainbow-hued heritage shophouses, hip cafes, and modern boutiques in 
  one of Singapore's most charming Eastern districts.

* 328 Katong Laksa
  Indulge in a piping hot bowl of laksa, a spicy coconut milk-based noodle soup 
  rich in local flavor.

* Kim Choo Kueh Chang
  A one-stop shop for discovering the rich tapestry of Peranakan culture, traditional 
  wares, and souvenirs.
