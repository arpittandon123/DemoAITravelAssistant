================================================================================
HONEYMOON TOUR IN SINGAPORE
================================================================================

Whether you're newly married or celebrating an anniversary, Singapore offers a 
dreamy backdrop of modern marvels, serene nature, world-class dining, and 
unforgettable romantic experiences for couples.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OVERVIEW & HIGHLIGHTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Ideal For: Newlyweds, couples, and anniversary celebrations.
* Highlights: Iconic city views, Michelin-starred fine dining, lush gardens, 
  luxury heritage hotels, and beachside relaxation.
* Key Highlights & Experiences:
  - Breathtaking skyline views and romantic evening light shows.
  - Fine dining at Michelin-starred and rooftop restaurants.
  - Peaceful strolls through UNESCO World Heritage sites and futuristic gardens.
  - Pampering couple spa sessions and luxury island retreats.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DAY 1: CIVIC DISTRICT, FINE DINING & SKYLINE VIEWS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* National Gallery Singapore
  Start your journey immersed in Southeast Asian art housed in Singapore's 
  historic City Hall and former Supreme Court buildings.

* Odette
  Enjoy a world-class, three-Michelin-starred fine dining lunch featuring 
  modern French cuisine inside the National Gallery.

* Gardens by the Bay
  Wander through the Flower Dome, Cloud Forest, and witness the mesmerizing 
  Supertree Grove light show (Garden Rhapsody) in the evening.

* Marguerite
  Experience romantic dining nestled within the lush greenery of the Flower Dome.

* Raffles Hotel & CÉ LA VI
   Sip on an iconic Singapore Sling at the historic Raffles Hotel, then end the 
   evening with drinks and panoramic skyline views at CÉ LA VI rooftop bar atop 
   Marina Bay Sands.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DAY 2: ISLAND ESCAPE & SENTOSA RELAXATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Cable Car Ride to Sentosa
  Take a scenic cable car ride offering 360-degree views of the harbor and island.

* Tanjong Beach & Coastal Lounges
  Relax on quiet sandy shores or kick back at a stylish beach club.

* Spa Pampering for Couples
  Unwind with custom massage treatments at luxury resorts like Sofitel Singapore 
  Sentosa Resort & Spa or Capella Singapore.

* Sunset Cruise & Waterfront Dining
  End the day with a private yacht charter or romantic seaside dinner in Quayside Isle.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DAY 3: HERITAGE, NATURE & ROMANTIC SPOTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Singapore Botanic Gardens & National Orchid Garden
  Take a quiet morning stroll through Singapore's UNESCO World Heritage site 
  and explore thousands of blooming orchid species.

* The Halia at Ginger Garden
  Enjoy brunch amidst lush tropical greenery in the heart of the Botanic Gardens.

* Kampong Gelam & Haji Lane
  Explore colorful shophouses, boutique stores, and vibrant murals together.

* Singapore River Cruise & Night Safari
  Take a traditional bumboat ride along the Singapore River, or embark on a nocturnal 
  wildlife adventure at the world-renowned Night Safari.
