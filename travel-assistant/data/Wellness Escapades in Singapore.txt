WELLNESS ESCAPADES IN SINGAPORE (RELAXING 4-DAY ITINERARY)
================================================================================

Find new ways to relax, rejuvenate and revitalise your body and spirit during 
your tropical vacation to the Lion City. Discover tranquil nature spots, 
holistic wellness clubs, serene island escapes, and iconic sights.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DAY 1: GARDENS AND HIP CAFES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Singapore Botanic Gardens
  Recognised as a UNESCO World Heritage Site, highlights include the National 
  Orchid Garden, a gorgeous swan lake, and tropical trees to take in the 
  beauty of nature.

* Prairie By Craftsmen
  Dig into delectable brunches, beverages, and desserts in this gorgeous 
  glasshouse cafe surrounded by nature and natural sunlight.

* Duxton Hill
  If you're looking to unwind, this neighbourhood is home to a range of hidden 
  gems. Find traditional shophouse architecture, quaint boutiques, and buzzing 
  cafes and bars.

* Trapeze Rec Club
  A one-stop holistic wellness club in the city. Discover four floors of 
  wellness classes from yoga and martial arts to recovery sessions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DAY 2: ENJOY A SLOWER PACE OF SINGAPORE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Bishan-Ang Mo Kio Park
  This 62-hectare park is home to a variety of fauna and flora, pond gardens, 
  and river plains, making it a must-visit for those looking for respite in 
  the city.

* COMO Shambhala
  A multi-storey lifestyle destination featuring a range of wellness and 
  fitness offerings, from mindfulness and sound healing to pilates classes.

* Dempsey Hill
  A lesser-known locale home to luxury shops and stellar dining options, this 
  heritage site is now an idyllic lifestyle destination that offers a slower 
  pace of life.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DAY 3: ESCAPE THE CITY (ISLAND GETAWAYS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Pulau Ubin & Mangrove Kayaking
  Nature and history intertwine on Pulau Ubin off Singapore's north-eastern coast. 
  Take a beginner-friendly guided kayak tour to explore tranquil mangroves and 
  abundant flora and fauna.

* Lazarus Island
  Head to Lazarus Island in the south for pristine beaches, quiet picnics, and 
  a tranquil island getaway.

* Dusit Thani Laguna
  Relax in your luxurious suite at Dusit Thani Laguna before teeing off on the 
  idyllic greens of the golf course and pampering yourself at Devarana Wellness.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DAY 4: SWING BY SOME OF THE CITY'S ICONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Buddha Tooth Relic Temple
  Located in the historic district of Chinatown, this majestic place of worship 
  is a repository of Buddhist wisdom and relics.

* ArtScience Museum
  Catch world-class touring exhibitions at this premier exhibition space, where 
  the wondrous worlds of art and science collide.

* Gardens by the Bay
  Revel in spectacular views from the Supertree Grove amid lush gardens, and 
  escape from the tropical heat inside the cooled conservatories.
