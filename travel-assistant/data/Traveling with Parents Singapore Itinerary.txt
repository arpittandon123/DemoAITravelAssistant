================================================================================
TRAVELING WITH PARENTS ITINERARY
================================================================================

Discover comfortable, accessible, and engaging itineraries designed specifically 
for family trips with aging parents or multi-generational travelers. Enjoy 
a balance of relaxed sightseeing, rich culture, gentle walks, and world-class 
dining with minimal walking strain and convenient transport options.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OVERVIEW & COMFORT TRAVEL HIGHLIGHTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Accessibility & Convenience:
  - Wheelchair and stroller-accessible public transit (MRT and buses).
  - Plentiful seating, elevators, and climate-controlled resting areas across 
    major attractions.
  - Comfortable taxi and private-hire ride options for door-to-door convenience.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DAY 1: HERITAGE, CULTURAL LANDMARKS & GENTLE WALKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Chinatown & Buddha Tooth Relic Temple
  Take a relaxed morning stroll through Chinatown. Visit the majestic Buddha 
  Tooth Relic Temple, equipped with elevator access across all floors.

* Asian Civilisations Museum
  Discover Southeast Asian heritage through accessible galleries and indoor 
  exhibits in the historic Civic District.

* Singapore River Bumboat Cruise
  Rest your feet while enjoying scenic views of Marina Bay Sands, the historic 
  bridges, and the skyline on a tranquil river cruise.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DAY 2: GARDENS, FLOWERS & SKYLINE VIEWS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Gardens by the Bay (Flower Dome & Cloud Forest)
  Explore temperature-controlled glass greenhouses featuring flat, barrier-free 
  walkways, ample seating, and exotic global flora.

* Floral Fantasy
  A gentle, scenic indoor walkthrough filled with suspended floral arrangements, 
  ideal for senior-friendly sightseeing.

* Marina Bay Sands SkyPark Observation Deck
  Enjoy high-altitude panoramic views of the city with elevator access and comfortable 
  viewing areas.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DAY 3: NATURE, TEA & TASTY RETREATS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Singapore Botanic Gardens
  Enjoy an early morning stroll along paved pathways in Singapore's UNESCO World 
  Heritage site, including the National Orchid Garden.

* Tea Appreciation at Tea Chapter
  Experience a quiet, traditional Chinese tea tasting session in Chinatown, 
  offering relaxation and cultural insight.

* Lau Pa Sat or Maxwell Food Centre
  Sample iconic Singaporean hawker dishes like Hainanese chicken rice, satay, and 
  fresh fruit juices with comfortable seated dining.
